**Title:** [Free] Neuzeit Drop control surface for Studio One 7

---

Sharing a free, open-source (MIT) Studio One 7 control-surface device for the **Neuzeit Instruments Drop**.

**Features**
- 4×4 pads → Launcher clip launch, with colour/state LED feedback
- Faders + mutes → volume/mute for 64 mixer channels across the Drop's 8 layers, with DAW→Drop feedback, and fader unity calibrated to 0.0 dB
- Encoders / encoder pushes → free Control Link knobs and buttons
- Arrow buttons → move the Launcher focus box, plus Stop-All
- Snapshots → launch any Launcher scene via Program Change

**Requirements:** Studio One 7, a Drop, and the included Drop config.
**Limitation:** no transport control. S1 7 can't follow incoming MIDI clock or Start/Stop.
